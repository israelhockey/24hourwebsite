Homepage is about PhoenixTitle, headline and description all say Phoenix. Their Google ranking is in Prescott Valley. A Prescott visitor has to find the small links to the Prescott Valley page.
Review gap4.6★ / 75 reviews against 2,355 for the #2 listing. That gap is the biggest ranking lever they have.

Fair to say: it's a modern WordPress site under the hood with decent titles, lazy-loaded images, Google and Facebook tracking and a Prescott Valley page. The weakness is look, layout and focus. Mobile speed not measured.

How Madeleine Coffee & Patisserie can get found by more people on Google

בית הקפה מדורג 4.6 ב-Google, אבל עם 11 ביקורות בלבד. בגלל זה הוא לא מופיע ב-20 התוצאות הראשונות בחיפושים כמו "בית קפה" ו-coffee shop ממש ליד השוק. בפרופיל Google אין אתר מקושר. הצעדים המהירים: להשלים את הפרופיל (אתר, תמונות, תפריט, שעות), לאסוף ביקורות עם קוד QR על השולחן, ולבנות אתר קטן בעברית ובאנגלית. המרפסת עם הנוף לים והשקיעה היא יתרון ייחודי, כדאי לשים אותה במרכז.

The short version. Customers love the place (4.6 stars), but Google barely knows it exists. Only 11 reviews, no website, and it did not show up in the top 20 cafes when we searched from its own street. The fix is mostly simple and fast: finish the Google profile, collect reviews, and put up a small website.

What we checked

1. Google Maps: who shows up for the searches that bring customers

We searched each term with the map centered on the cafe's own street. Madeleine did not appear in the top 20 for any of them. These cafes did, and almost all have far more reviews:

The rating is not the problem. Google ranks cafes with more reviews, more photos and a complete profile higher.

2. Website and what shows up when people search the name

Searching the cafe's name shows delivery and listing sites (Wolt, Wanderlog, Restaurantguru, Atly) and Instagram, but no site of its own. The Google profile has no website link. Searching "best cafe near Carmel Market" returns local guides and listings (easy.co.il, shuktlv.co.il, TripAdvisor, Time Out), and Madeleine is not among them.

3. Name and listing

The cafe appears as Madeleine Coffee & Patisserie on Google, מדלן פטיסרי on Instagram, and customers say "Madlen". Google also shows a separate entry, מדלן בתי קפה בע"מ, with no reviews. It may be unrelated, but it should be checked, because a duplicate or confusing listing splits reviews and traffic.

4. What customers already say

Reviews mention the breakfast platter, the tuna and avocado sandwiches, strong tasty coffee, friendly service, and a balcony with a sea view at sunset. That balcony is something almost no other cafe at the market can offer, and nothing online says so yet.

What we would do

Week 1Fix the Google profile (fastest win)
  • Add the website, menu, full opening hours (including holidays) and the right categories (coffee shop, patisserie/bakery).
  • Upload 25+ good photos: the food, the coffee, the counter, the balcony and the sea view at sunset, the market outside.
  • Use one name everywhere: add the Hebrew name מדלן פטיסרי to the English name and check the second listing.
  • Reply to every review and post an update weekly.
Week 1Get more reviews
  • A small QR code on every table and the receipt that opens the Google review box.
  • Staff asks happy customers. Goal: 50+ reviews in about 60 days.
Weeks 2–3One-page website, Hebrew and English
  • Menu, hours, map, phone, WhatsApp button, Wolt link, photos, and the balcony view up front.
  • Set up so Google understands it is a cafe at Daniel St 25 (local business data, correct address and phone everywhere).
  • Fast on a phone, since most searches come from phones.
Month 1–2Be listed where people look
  • Claim or update the cafe on TripAdvisor, the Carmel Market guide (shuktlv.co.il), Time Out, easy.co.il and Restaurantguru, all with the same name, address and phone.
  • Instagram: link the website in the bio, tag the location, keep posting reels of the food and the view.
OngoingTrack it
  • Every month, re-check where the cafe ranks for "cafe Carmel Market", "breakfast Tel Aviv" and "בית קפה שוק הכרמל", plus calls, direction requests and website visits from the Google profile.

What this should lead to

More people finding the cafe when they search near the market, more calls and walk-ins, and more reviews, which keeps pushing it higher. We can't promise a ranking, because Google decides, but these are the steps that move cafes up.

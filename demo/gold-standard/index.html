Questions we get

How much does weekly pool service cost in Glendale?

It depends on pool size, water condition and equipment. Use our monthly price estimator for a quick estimate, then we confirm it in person.

What is included in weekly pool service?

Debris removal, brushing walls, steps and landings, emptying baskets, an equipment check with small repairs, chemical dosing, and an emailed report with a photo.

Can you service a green pool?

Yes. Tell us the water condition in the estimator. Black swamp water must be drained before service can start, and we can help arrange that.

Do you repair and install pool equipment?

Yes. We repair leaks and filter or cleaner problems, patch PebbleTec, replace small parts, and install pumps, filters and salt cells.
